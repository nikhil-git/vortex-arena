🎮 About Vortex Arena — 3D Multiplayer Online Gun Shooting Game

Vortex Arena is a fast-paced, browser-native 12-player 3D FPS/TPS multiplayer shooter powered by modern WebGL (Three.js) and real-time authoritative WebSockets. Hosted 24/7 on high-speed cloud servers, Vortex Arena delivers responsive 60 FPS combat directly in your web browser with zero client installs required across laptops, desktops, and mobile devices.

⚡ Key Game Features:

  • Real-Time 12-Player Shooter: Instant public matchmaking and custom private matches with up to 12 simultaneous players per arena.
  • Tactical Weapons Arsenal: Master the V-44 Striker Assault Rifle, Hawk-50 Sniper Rifle with optical ADS scope, Breacher-12 Shotgun, and Pulse-9 Pistol.
  • Server-Authoritative Anti-Cheat: Validated raycast hit registration, kinetic shield absorption, and dynamic damage feedback.
  • Friends & Social System: Add friends with unique #ID tags, view live online/match statuses, and send squad match invitations.
  • Milestone Progression & Skins: Unlock permanent achievements and cosmetic weapon/character skins at 10, 50, and 60,000 confirmed kills.
  • Cross-Platform WebGL Engine: Instant play on Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, Android APK, and Apple iOS Home Screen PWA.
