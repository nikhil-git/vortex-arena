VORTEX ARENA — PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: November 4, 2026 | Last Updated: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vortex Arena ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, protects, and handles your information when you access or play the Vortex Arena video game across Android mobile applications, desktop web browsers, and supported gaming platforms.

1. Information We Collect

We prioritize player privacy and minimize data collection to what is strictly necessary to deliver real-time multiplayer matches:

  • Account Information: When you choose to create a registered account (optional), we collect a public username and an encrypted password hash. An optional email address may be provided solely for account recovery. Guest players use a temporary pseudonymous player identifier.
  • Gameplay & Progression Statistics: We store in-game stats such as lifetime kills, matches played, accuracy, damage dealt, leaderboard rankings, and unlocked cosmetic skins.
  • Technical & Network Data: To facilitate low-latency multiplayer synchronization and matchmaking, our servers process IP addresses, connection ping/latency, and device platform type (Android, Windows, macOS). This information is processed in transient memory.
  • Crash & Performance Logs: In the event of an unexpected crash or disconnection, anonymous technical logs (OS version, GPU renderer type, error stack trace) may be collected to identify bugs.

2. How We Use Information

Collected information is used exclusively for:

  • Authoritative real-time multiplayer matchmaking and 12-player combat synchronization.
  • Saving and displaying your player progression, milestones, and cosmetic rewards.
  • Preventing cheating, bot exploitation, and abusive behavior in multiplayer lobbies.
  • Optimizing game performance and frame-rate stability across different Android screen resolutions.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do NOT sell, lease, or monetize your personal or gameplay data to third-party data brokers or behavioral advertisers. Data is shared only with essential infrastructure providers:

  • Cloud Hosting & WebSockets: Cloud hosting infrastructure (Render, PostgreSQL) used to execute the authoritative game server under strict encryption (TLS/WSS).

4. Data Security

All network communication between the game client and backend servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS / WSS). Passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed. We enforce strict access controls on all production databases.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your gameplay data for as long as your account remains active. You have the unconditional right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated statistics at any time:

To request account deletion, visit our self-service page at https://vortex-arena-1nmx.onrender.com/account-deletion or submit a request to support with your registered username.

6. Children's Privacy

Vortex Arena does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact the development team at:
Email: support@vortex-arena.com
